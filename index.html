FIELD TIP

Fact or folklore?

A legend can tell us a lot about a place's culture, even when the story itself isn't proven history.

Ask: who recorded this story, when was it written down, and is there independent evidence? A legend is part of local culture—not automatically a verified event.

Missions are designed for family exploration. The app never requires running, entering restricted areas, or approaching strangers.

WELCOME, EXPLORER

Find the stories around you

World Hunter uses your location to centre the map and find nearby points of interest. Your GPS position stays on this device; approximate coordinates are sent to public map and article services to retrieve nearby results.

Choose “Enable location” to continue. Your browser or Android will show its own permission prompt. You can change location access later in browser settings.
✦

Explorer settings

The map frames an area about 3× this radius. You can discover a point from further away, but visiting it is recorded only when you're within 35 m. GPS accuracy varies, especially between tall buildings.

Location is used on this device while the app is open. This MVP does not send your GPS position to an account or tracking server. Nearby-place lookups send approximate coordinates to public map/data services when you request them.
